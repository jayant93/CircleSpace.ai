✦ About Our AI

Technology that listens
so humans can heal

At CircleSpace.ai, we believe the right group can change everything — but only if the match is truly right. Traditional intake forms miss the nuance. Questionnaires miss the emotion. So we built something better.

Your private consultation is securely transcribed and processed by our AI model, which captures not just your words, but the themes, emotions, and readiness signals beneath them. The result: a placement that feels like it was made for you — because it was.

🎙️

Private Session Transcription

Your intake call is transcribed with end-to-end encryption. No human reads the raw transcript — only the AI model processes it for matching purposes, then it is anonymized.

🤖

Intelligent Therapy Matching

The AI evaluates emotional language, stated concerns, communication style, and session signals to recommend the therapy modality and group cohort where you will make the most progress.

🔄

Adaptive Re-Matching

As your needs evolve over time, the system can recommend transitioning you to a different group or modality — ensuring your care always fits where you actually are, not where you started.

🛡️

Clinician Oversight — Always

AI is a decision-support layer, not a replacement for clinical judgment. Every group placement is reviewed and confirmed by a licensed therapist before you receive your match.

🔒 CircleSpace.ai is HIPAA-aligned and built with a privacy-first architecture. All transcription data is anonymized post-matching and never retained beyond minimum legal requirements.
